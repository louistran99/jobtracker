Between Sessions — Mock Design v1

A between-session companion for therapy clients: carry the Intentions you set with your therapist through the week, check in on them, and read your session recap. Six key screens below, iPhone 16 Pro size (393 × 852 pt). Comment by screen number, e.g. “3: drop the chart.”

Open questions for you

  1. Tabs: This Week / Sessions / Settings — or fold Settings into the avatar and add a “Progress” tab?
  2. Mood scale: 5 faces (Rough → Great) on every check-in, or keep check-ins to a single tap and make mood optional?
  3. Sharing: check-ins shared with the therapist by default (screen 2 toggle on), or private by default?
  4. Notification privacy: I defaulted previews to Generic (“Time for a check-in”); screen 5 shows the opt-in Detailed version. Agree with that default?
  5. Scope: is the “For next time” topic list on the recap (screen 4) worth the hours, or cut it?
